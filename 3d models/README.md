# S5 gantry clamp

Open these STL files directly in your slicer. No Python or CAD setup needed.

## Print order

1. Print the four `check_*.stl` files first. Check the nuts, both bearings and the motor-hole pattern against your actual parts.
2. Once they fit, print one each: `centre_body.stl`, `upper_cap.stl` and `lower_cap.stl`. Review supports in the slicer, especially the centre body's downward-facing bearing seat and the screw recesses.

## Hardware

- 8 × M3×15 mm round-head bolts for the bearing caps.
- 4 × M3×20 mm round-head bolts for the motor mount.
- 12 × ordinary M3 hex nuts (5.5 mm across flats, about 2.4 mm thick). Not nyloc or flange nuts.
- 12 × M3 flat washers (7 mm outside diameter, 0.3–0.8 mm thick).

Bunnings packs: [M3×15 bolts and nuts](https://www.bunnings.com.au/pinnacle-m3-x-15mm-zinc-plated-round-head-bolts-and-nuts-20-pack_p0168388), [M3×20 bolts and nuts](https://www.bunnings.com.au/pinnacle-m3-x-20mm-zinc-plated-round-head-bolts-and-nuts-18-pack_p0247264), [washers](https://www.bunnings.com.au/pinnacle-m3-black-flat-washer-12-pack_p0257725). Buy one of each bolt pack and two washer packs for spares. Check Campbelltown stock before going.

## Assembly and fit

Slide nuts into the side slots. Use four 15 mm bolts per cap, with a washer under each head. Tighten each cap separately and gently; the rods must still slide freely. Attach the existing motor bracket to the four fixed feet using the 20 mm bolts and washers, from underneath. Motor bolts must not pull the two caps together.

**Prototype: fit and drill-load strength are untested.** Bearing spacing is 18 mm. Motor-hole spacing is an assumed **36×40 mm**: check the gauge before printing the main parts or drilling anything. The motor mounting face is 16 mm below the lower rod centre; reset tool height and check full travel. Motor bolts assume an accessible 2–10 mm thick mounting flange. Stop if holes do not align, bolts bottom out, plastic cracks or rods bind. Do not force a fit or use for unattended drilling.
