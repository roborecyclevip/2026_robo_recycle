## Check in the shop

- M3 metric **0.5 mm pitch** machine bolts, not timber/self-tapping screws, not countersunk heads.
- Ordinary M3 hex nuts, nominal **5.5 mm across flats and 2.4 mm thick**. Do not substitute taller nyloc, flange or cap nuts: they will not fit these pockets. Exact Pinnacle nut envelope is not published on the linked listing; measure/check the purchased nuts with the included coupon before the main print.
- Washers must fit the 8.2 mm counterbores. The design calculation uses 0.5 mm thickness; 0.3–0.8 mm is the checked clamp-stack range. If the purchased washers are thicker, change the stack/recess before assembly rather than losing nut engagement.
- Obtain a small screwdriver/bit that snugly fits the actual supplied bolt heads. Do not assume the original S5 hex driver fits these round-head bolts.

## Useful extras, only if you do not already have them

- **150 mm digital or vernier caliper:** strongly useful for measuring the nut pockets, 18 mm middle section and actual mounting pattern; no brand/stock/price confirmed here.
- Small tweezers or needle-nose pliers to load nuts through the side slots.
- 3.5 mm drill bit for gently clearing printed M3 holes and, only after fit verification, enlarging existing *plastic* M2.5 clearance holes where adequate wall remains. Clamp the removed part; do not drill near installed bearings, rods, motor or wiring. Do not enlarge an original threaded metal hole on assumption.
- Fine file/deburring tool and normal eye protection for drilling/support removal.

Do not buy threadlocker or glue for the printed nut traps. Captive pockets prevent nut rotation during tightening but are not vibration-locking nuts. Inspect witness marks and preload during testing; this prototype is not approved for unattended drill operation.
